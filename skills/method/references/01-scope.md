# 1. Frame the team's scope

## What it gives you

A scope expressed as jobs and outcomes rather than features or opinions. Everyone works on the same customer problems, so goals line up across disciplines and the most valuable work rises to the top. Because the scope already names the outcomes to improve, the team also gets its success metrics for free and can track impact over time.

## Steps

1. Get the whole team in the room (meeting or workshop).
2. Walk through the knowledge base together and choose the performer or performers you serve.
3. Choose the main jobs you will work on.
4. Within them, choose the job steps you will address.
5. For those jobs and steps, choose the outcomes you intend to move.

## Running it with Claude

Claude lists, for a performer, the main jobs, ordered steps and outcomes with their status and opportunity score, so the team picks from evidence. It then saves the chosen scope to `outputs/` (and as a page under the hub on the Notion backend).
