# 13. Position the solution with product marketing

## Idea

Use the job statements and the value propositions that won to shape positioning, the key product marketing messages and the distribution strategy. Once go-to-market timing and goals are set, pick the main channels, then write copy and content for each one that carries both the functional and the emotional side of the job.

## Proposed steps (to validate in practice)

1. Choose the main job and its most underserved outcomes.
2. Set them against the alternatives the performer relies on today.
3. Write three messages: one functional, one emotional, one social.
4. Match each message to channels and to moments in the job.
