# 4. Recruit participants by the job they do

## What it gives you

Selecting people on the job they are trying to get done, not on demographics or job titles, puts you in front of the people who actually live the problem. Findings are more accurate and easier to act on, and you learn the real motivations and obstacles behind what people do.

## Steps

1. Choose the job or desired outcome that best captures your users' main priority.
2. Check with each candidate whether they actually do that job or care about that outcome (by email, an in-product survey, or a screener).
3. Keep only the candidates who match.

## Running it with Claude

Claude writes the screener from the selected jobs: questions about recent behavior ("In the last month, how often did you..."), the alternative they use today, disqualifiers, and the target mix of participants.
