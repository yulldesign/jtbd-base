# 14. Measure the change in satisfaction after launch

## What it gives you

A structured way to check whether the product really serves users' core needs. Measuring on jobs and outcomes shows how well the solution delivers, which improvements count, and which needs are still unmet, so the next iteration stays anchored on real expectations.

## Steps

1. Prerequisite: satisfaction was measured at the start of the project (the survey of method 8 does it).
2. Prepare a survey on satisfaction for the jobs or outcomes the solution targets.
3. Send it a few weeks after the release.
4. Compare the answers with the baseline.

## Running it with Claude

Claude stores the new values in Satisfaction post-launch and reports the delta for each outcome. Without a baseline it says so and proposes method 8 first.
