# 12. Share results in terms of jobs and outcomes

## What it gives you

Talking about the job people are trying to get done and the outcomes they expect keeps the message on the value delivered, not on the features shipped. Stakeholders, customers and the team see how the product meets real needs, agree on what it is for, and understand the impact of the project.

## Steps

1. Open with the job users are trying to get done and what success means to them (their desired outcomes).
2. Speak the users' everyday language and show how the product helps them reach those outcomes, rather than listing features.
3. Back it with concrete examples or case studies of users getting the job done.
4. Link the message to measured outcomes, for example time saved or satisfaction gained.
