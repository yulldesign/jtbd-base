# 15. Keep the jobs visible throughout the project

## What it gives you

Everyone, developers included, keeps sight of the real problems users are trying to solve. The product's purpose stays clear beyond the technical requirements, and decisions stay tied to user goals.

## Steps

1. Lay out the jobs on a board: for each performer, the roles it covers and its aspiration; then for each main job, its emotional job, its social job and its ordered steps.
2. Use this board as the reference in every team moment: brainstorms, presentations, mockup reviews, kick-offs.

## Running it with Claude

Ask Claude for a performer's map: it renders the board from the base, ready to paste or share.
