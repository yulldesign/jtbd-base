# 10. Generate ideas with How Might We questions

## Idea

Reframe jobs as How Might We questions ([d.school guide](https://dschool.stanford.edu/resources/how-might-we-questions)) so the problems the product must solve are stated sharply enough to ideate on.

## Proposed steps (to validate in practice)

1. Choose an underserved outcome (high importance, low satisfaction).
2. Reword it as "How might we help [performer] [outcome] when [job step]?"
3. Write several variants (broader, narrower, flipped) and keep the ones that spark the most ideas.
