# 2. Expand the scope in a team brainstorm

## What it gives you

Looking for finer sub-jobs and outcomes as a team builds empathy for the people you serve and a sharper picture of what they struggle with. It refines the scope, adds hypotheses nobody had written down, and surfaces opportunities that were being missed, so later work is better aimed.

## Steps

1. Gather the whole team (meeting or workshop).
2. Start by restating the scope: performers, jobs, outcomes.
3. Generate more specific sub-jobs together.
4. Generate new desired outcomes, for the jobs you already had and for the new ones.
5. Review the results as a group and rewrite what needs it.
6. Lay out a customer journey map made of the existing and new jobs, in order.
