# 7. Map the real competition for each job

## What it gives you

The competition users experience is rarely the one on a market slide. They get the job done with whatever works: internal tools, custom builds, workarounds, manual effort, or nothing at all. Every one of these competes with you, and every one is a source of ideas for the solution you design.

## Ways to find it

Use one or several, and always map competition job by job.

- **In interviews:** ask how they get the job done today, then ask why that way.
- **In analysis:** look for indirect clues of how they get it done, then dig into the reasons.
- **From known competitors:** study which jobs and outcomes their products address, and how.
- **With a survey:** have users rate their satisfaction on specific jobs or outcomes for your solution and for each alternative.
